Impacto de acerto universal — texturas para gerar no canvas da Tripo

Frente: VFX e magia (docs/frentes/vfx-magia.md). Data: 2026-11-19. Motivo: cinco voltas com textura feita em código não passaram no crivo (núcleo de 33 px, faísca de 1–2 px). As texturas abaixo substituem as de código em ImpactVfxPlayer.

Regras para todas

  • Quadrado 1:1, exportar no maior tamanho (2048 px ou mais), PNG.
  • Fundo preto puro #000000, sem chão, sem personagem, sem texto, sem moldura, sem marca-d'água.
  • Forma branca / cinza-claro (a cor do elemento é aplicada no jogo: uma textura serve aos 9 elementos). Só a nº 6 é colorida.
  • Forma centrada, com folga de ~10% até a borda (nada cortado).
  • Estilo: pintado à mão, traço grosso e legível de longe (Diablo IV / Lost Ark), não fotorrealista, sem lens flare.
  • Gerar 4 variações de cada e salvar em [removido] com o nome indicado (_a, _b...).

1. Imagem-alvo (referência, não vai para o jogo) — alvo_impacto.png

Isometric action RPG screenshot, top-down 45 degree camera, a warrior's sword striking a goblin, at the contact point a bold hit impact effect: bright white-hot starburst flash about the size of the goblin's torso, 6 to 8 thick chunky sparks flying outward in a cone away from the attacker, tiny dust puff at the feet, dark fantasy meadow ground, stylized hand-painted game art in the style of Diablo IV and Lost Ark, high contrast, readable silhouette, no UI, no text

2. Clarão de contato — clarao.png

Game VFX texture, single hit impact flash, asymmetric starburst with 5 thick tapered spikes of uneven length and a solid bright core, hand-painted stylized, pure white and light gray on pure black background (#000000), centered, soft falloff at the tips, no lens flare, no rings, no text, for additive rendering, square

3. Estilhaços / faíscas (grade 2×2) — faiscas_2x2.png

Game VFX sprite sheet, 2x2 grid of four different spark streak sprites, each a thick tapered comet-shaped streak pointing up, bright head and fading tail, slightly bent, chunky and readable, hand-painted stylized, pure white on pure black background (#000000), each sprite centered in its cell with margin, no overlap between cells, no grid lines, no text, for additive rendering, square

4. Explosão animada (grade 4×4, 16 quadros) — impacto_flipbook_4x4.png

Game VFX flipbook sprite sheet, 4x4 grid, 16 sequential frames read left to right and top to bottom of a single melee hit impact burst: frame 1 small bright core, frames 2-4 starburst expanding fast with thick spikes, frames 5-10 spikes breaking into chunky shards flying outward, frames 11-16 shards thinning and fading to black, same center in every cell, hand-painted stylized, pure white and light gray on pure black background (#000000), no grid lines, no text, for additive rendering, square

5. Golpe resistido (desvio) — resistido_arco.png

Game VFX texture, deflected blow glint, a single thick curved arc like a shield edge catching light, crescent shape opening downward, bright in the middle and fading at both ends, two tiny dull sparks bouncing off, hand-painted stylized, pure white and gray on pure black background (#000000), centered, no text, for additive rendering, square

6. Brasas (única colorida, grade 2×2) — brasas_2x2.png

Game VFX sprite sheet, 2x2 grid of four different glowing ember sprites, irregular small burning coal fragments with a hot amber core (#F2B347) and forge-orange edge (#E8742A), short flame lick on each, hand-painted stylized, on pure black background (#000000), each centered in its cell with margin, no grid lines, no text, for additive rendering, square

7. Poeira de chão (alfa, para chão claro) — poeira_2x2.png

Game VFX sprite sheet, 2x2 grid of four different small dust puff sprites, soft billowy cloud shapes with clear painted edges, hand-painted stylized, light gray on pure black background (#000000), each centered in its cell with margin, no grid lines, no text, square

Ordem de prioridade

1 (alvo) → 2 → 3 → 5 primeiro; 4, 6 e 7 em seguida. Com 2, 3 e 5 já dá para refazer a volta 6.

O que acontece depois

Recorte das grades por script, brilho vira alfa para a nº 7, troca no ImpactVfxPlayer, captura a 60 fps nos dois chãos e crivo independente.

Volta 9 — peças novas pedidas pelo crivo da v8 (laudo-crivo-v8.md)

Mesmas regras do topo (1:1, preto puro, branco, forma solta, 15% de folga, 4 variações, nó com Número = 4, prompt no singular). Salvar em [removido] com o nome indicado + _a.._d.

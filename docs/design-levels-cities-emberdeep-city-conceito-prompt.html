1. Foto da cidade (vista isometrica alta), criar em 2K, 16:9

``text Isometric high-angle concept art of a dwarven forge city carved in three terraced rings around a huge never-dying furnace, a spiral street, a chain elevator, glowing orange windows, dark iron roofs, amber smoke, for an action RPG. Camera about 50 degrees above the ground, looking diagonally down at the whole place (320 x 280 metres), north toward the top of the picture. Visual direction "Dark Priston" v3: aged, desaturated earthy palette (olive #74784A, packed earth #8A7458, warm stone #7F796C, aged brass #8C6C3E, dark timber #5E4632), warm late-afternoon light from the upper left with long soft shadows, heavy stone, timber and iron with wear, rivets and moss; hand-painted stylized look with clean readable low-poly-friendly shapes. Cool crystal blue (#8FB8D0) only as small accents. Atmospheric depth in layers: farther planes lighter, cooler and lower contrast. Landmark: the never-dying furnace in the centre. On the far northern horizon, behind the distant ridge, a dark crystal spire silhouette (body #272F4E, faint blue veins #4F73BC) whose top barely rises (about 37.8 degrees), softened by haze. Show the services as readable buildings with distinct silhouettes (shops, smithy, inn, healer, bank, guild hall, as in the plan). Ways out: open gateways on the map edges as in the plan. A few tiny figures for scale only. No text, no letters, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no pure black, no pure white, no neon, no saturated orange or red on the ground. ``

Negativo / evitar: modern buildings, cartoon candy colors, pastel pink or mint, snow, fog wall that hides the mountains, text, UI, harsh black shadows, photographic realism, characters larger than a few pixels.

2. Variante aerea para a planta, editar o resultado do passo 1, 1K, 1:1 ou 4:3

``text Edit of the approved isometric concept: same city, same layout and same buildings, but redrawn as a straight top-down orthographic plan view (camera looking straight down, north up), no perspective, flat even light with only soft contact shadows. Keep every street, plaza, bridge, wall and landmark in the same relative position and proportion (the never-dying furnace in the centre). Clean readable rooftop footprints and clear walkable ground, so it can be compared 1:1 with the engineering plan. Same palette and Dark Priston ageing. No text, no UI, no grid lines, no characters. ``

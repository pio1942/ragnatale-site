SILHOUETTE: a bell of robe widening to the ground, with a tall staff topped by a hook that rises well above the head. POSE: upright and slightly leaning back, staff held far to one side, other hand raised with a painted rune on the palm, as if reading in the air. PERSONALITY: curious wide eyes, ink-stained fingers, a pencil tucked behind the ear. OUTFIT: floor-length robe that hides the legs and feet, rune stripes, belt of scrolls. COLORS: 60 percent deep indigo, 30 percent pale cyan, small accents of gold.
